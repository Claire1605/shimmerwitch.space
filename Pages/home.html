Claire's Bio


Claire is a self-taught game designer, programmer, artist, and musician. Much of her work combines the physical and digital, and she is interested in things that are tactile, and that inspire appreciation of the natural world. Her artwork often contains hand-crafted styles such as claymation.

Claire is also interested in games as a medium for creative personal expression, and is fascinated by game development tools such as Bitsy, which provide accessible ways for people with no previous game development experience to make and share their games.

She has organised and run many events for people to learn how to make games, and is also very enthusiastic about running community events such as game jams and drop-in playful workshops.

In 2025 she released Asterism, an interactive music album video game set in space.

Prior to this she worked on Before I Forget with her company 3-Fold Games, a narrative exploration about a woman, Sunita, living with early-onset dementia.

Claire has exhibited her games and spoken about her work across the UK and internationally, as well as teaching on game development courses.

She is a member and former director of Biome Collective.

(anti-)AI statement


I am a human artist, and am very opposed to AI and the ongoing expansion of monopoly tech and hyperscale data centres. I do not use AI in my work, and am taking measures to shift away from monopoly tech software, devices, and accounts. Please do not get in touch about potential work if you use AI within your project.
