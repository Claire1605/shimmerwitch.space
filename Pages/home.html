Claire is a self-taught game designer, programmer, artist, and musician. In 2025 she released Asterism, an interactive music album video game set in space.

Prior to this she worked on Before I Forget with her company 3-Fold Games, a narrative exploration about a woman, Sunita, living with early-onset dementia, in which the player learns about Sunita's life by interacting with objects throughout her house and seeing her thoughts and memories that relate to them.

Claire is interested in games as a medium for creative personal expression, and is fascinated by game development tools such as Bitsy, which provide accessible ways for people with no previous game development experience to make and share their games. She runs events and workshops for people to learn how to make games, and is also very enthusiastic about running community events such as game jams.

She also works as an artist and is particularly interested in hand-crafted styles such as claymation, and enjoys experimenting with new art within games.
